**Answer Rule:**
- Yes = Good
- No = Problem found
- N/A = Not applicable

## AOUT Checklist Quality

- **A – Atomic:** Each question checks one point.
- **O – Observable:** Answers can be checked from the SRS.
- **U – Unambiguous:** Questions have clear wording.
- **T – Traceable:** Each question has a reason or source.

**Note:** Yes is always the good answer.
